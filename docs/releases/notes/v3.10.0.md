# SecInterp v3.10.0: Interactive Preview Legend

**SecInterp v3.10.0** moves the legend out of the map canvas into a dedicated, collapsible
side panel and makes it interactive: hide, recolor and read the units directly next to the
section, with drillhole lithologies joining the geology.

## 🗂️ Preview Side Panel

- The legend no longer covers the profile: it lives in a **collapsible side panel** beside the
  preview, together with a list of the drawn **interpretations**.
- A splitter lets you balance the canvas and panel, and the layout is remembered between
  sessions.

## 🎨 Per-Unit Legend Controls

- **Hide/show** and **change the color** of each geological unit from the panel; the choice is
  applied to the preview **and** its exported image, and is persisted.
- Structures and drillholes are unaffected by unit controls.

## 🕳️ Drillhole Lithologies in the Legend

- The drillhole **interval lithologies** now appear in the preview legend (and the exported
  image) alongside geology, so subsurface units can be interpreted. A "Drillholes" entry
  represents the traces.
